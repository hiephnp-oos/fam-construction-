# Construction estimating

## Objective
Produce a reproducible estimate whose scope, quantity basis, rate basis, assumptions and evidence can be reviewed independently.

## Workflow
1. **Clarify estimate purpose.** Budgetary, tender, variation, comparison or cost check; identify location, pricing date, currency, tax basis, accuracy expectation and required output.
2. **Define scope and unit.** Confirm item description, specification, quality, work method, measurement rule, included operations, interfaces and exclusions. Distinguish supply-only, installation-only and complete installed rate.
3. **Establish quantity provenance.** Link quantity to BOQ, explicit dimensions, verified measurement engine or manual trace. Record drawing/revision/sheet, scale calibration, method, geometry, formula, deductions and status. Do not price an unverified visual guess as fact.
4. **Select measurement approach.** Prefer dimension-based calculations or deterministic measurement tools for suitable geometry. For complex/irregular/obscured geometry, break into traceable parts or flag human measurement. Apply independent spot checks to material quantities.
5. **Build unit rate.** Show materials, documented wastage, labour/productivity, equipment, transport/handling/disposal, subcontract, site/company overhead, risk/profit and tax as applicable. Avoid double counting; identify inclusions in supplier quotes.
6. **Research current rates when needed.** Search current, local and comparable evidence. Prioritize dated supplier/contractor quotations and official publications. Record source, date, geography, specification, unit, tax and inclusions. Distinguish market observation from calculated estimate. Never invent a quote or imply a historical rate is current.
7. **Calculate and reconcile.** Show formulas and conversions; check dimensional consistency, subtotals and total = rate × quantity. Keep measured quantity, allowance and wastage separate.
8. **Run sensitivity/uncertainty check.** Identify dominant cost drivers, missing scope, uncertain productivity, access, quantity and market volatility. Use a range or provisional status when evidence does not support precision.
9. **Present and qualify.** State basis, inclusions/exclusions, assumptions, evidence status/date, uncertainty and items requiring confirmation.

## Required output
Compact itemized table: description | unit | quantity and basis | rate | amount | rate source/status. Follow with build-up, assumptions, exclusions, risk/uncertainty and unresolved questions.

## Guardrails
- Do not mix material-only and installed prices.
- Do not silently infer missing scope, dimensions, productivity or market prices.
- Do not imply a takeoff engine or plugin was used unless it was actually used.
- Indicative estimates must be labelled as such; precision in arithmetic does not remove uncertainty in inputs.
