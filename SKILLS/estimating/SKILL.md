# Construction estimating
## Trigger
Unit-rate estimates, cost build-ups, and construction pricing.
## Method
1. Confirm description, unit, quantity, location, date/basis, scope, and quality.
2. Define the measurable unit and calculate quantity per unit.
3. Separate materials, labor, equipment, transport, wastage, subcontracting, and other applicable direct costs.
4. Identify whether prices are supplied, sourced, historical, or assumed. Never fabricate a current quote.
5. Show formulas, assumptions, inclusions/exclusions, and taxes/overhead/profit where relevant.
6. Check dimensional consistency and total = unit rate × quantity.
## Output
Compact cost table with basis, unit price, amount, and source/status; then assumptions, exclusions, and uncertainty.
## Guardrails
Do not mix material-only and installed prices. Distinguish ready-mix from site-mixed concrete, and supply from installation. Without location/date/source, label the estimate indicative.
