# Contract analysis
## Trigger
Contract scope, payment, variation, delay, defects, acceptance, and risk review.
## Method
1. Identify contract/version, jurisdiction if stated, clause and page.
2. Quote or accurately paraphrase operative wording.
3. Separate explicit obligation, interpretation, assumption, and missing information.
4. Explain practical implications, notice/evidence/deadline/approval, and cost/time effects where supported.
5. Check definitions, precedence, notice, variation, payment, and dispute clauses.
6. Do not present legal advice or infer enforceability without qualified jurisdiction-specific review.
## Output
Clause/reference | requirement | practical implication | risk/ambiguity | action/evidence needed.
