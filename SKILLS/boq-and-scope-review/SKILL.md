# BOQ and scope review

## Objective
Determine whether BOQ descriptions and quantities have support in the issued project information. A matching quantity does not prove scope completeness.

## Workflow
1. **Freeze the review basis.** Inventory BOQ, drawings, specifications, addenda, clarifications and schedules. Record document number, revision/date, issue status and source. Identify gaps and conflicting revisions before comparison.
2. **Build a scope map.** Group by work package/location/system; map each BOQ line to drawing/spec/clause/schedule evidence. Track interfaces, preliminaries, temporary works, testing, commissioning, protection, disposal, maintenance and handover when relevant.
3. **Normalize for comparison.** Compare description, unit, quantity, material/performance, dimensions, location, inclusions/exclusions and measurement rule. Do not compare quantities with incompatible units or scope boundaries.
4. **Check quantity provenance.** Classify each quantity as: stated in BOQ; explicit dimension calculation; measured with tool; manual trace; derived allowance; or unverified. Record source sheet/page, scale/calibration, method, formula, geometry/region, deductions and reviewer status. Never present an AI visual estimate as a verified takeoff.
5. **Use measurement decision gates.** For simple regular geometry with legible scale, use explicit dimensions or a measurement engine and independently spot-check. For irregular, layered, curved, congested, obscured, low-resolution or multi-sheet geometry, split into verifiable regions or flag for human measurement. Do not force a single total from uncertain geometry.
6. **Reconcile.** Check missing, duplicate, quantity difference, unit mismatch, description/spec mismatch, location mismatch, revision mismatch, scope/interface gap, and unclear measurement basis. Distinguish confirmed discrepancy from potential discrepancy.
7. **Assess impact.** State likely cost, compliance, programme or responsibility impact only where supported. Do not decide document precedence unless the contract/tender explicitly establishes it.
8. **Resolve and recheck.** Formulate specific clarification questions. When an addendum/answer arrives, identify affected BOQ lines, drawing references, quantity calculations and pricing assumptions; do not overwrite the audit trail.

## Required output
A discrepancy register with: ID | BOQ reference | drawing/spec reference | revision | issue/category | evidence | quantity basis/status | impact | clarification/action. Add a short coverage statement: documents reviewed, excluded/unreadable sheets, and scope areas not confirmed.

## Acceptance gate
Do not label a BOQ “fully checked” unless the reviewed document set and coverage are stated, each material line is mapped or explicitly unmapped, quantity basis is classified, and unresolved/high-risk items are visible. Human confirmation remains required for uncertain measurements and scope decisions.
