# Construction fundamentals
Stable reusable fundamentals only; project-specific requirements remain with the project.
- Read relevant plan, section, detail, legend, specification, and revision.
- Keep units consistent and show calculation basis.
- Distinguish design intent, means/methods, and actual site conditions.
- Connect function, operating principle, physical phenomenon, consequence, and drawing/site evidence.
- Verify standards, editions, and local requirements before citing; do not rely on memory for clause numbers.
- Record uncertainty explicitly; missing data is not permission to assume.
